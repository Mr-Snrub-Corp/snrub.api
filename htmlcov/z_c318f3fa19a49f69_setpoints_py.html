1"""Per-metric warning/danger bands, extracted from docs/telemetry.md prose. 

2 

3Single shared source of truth consumed by alarm evaluation now 

4(services/alarms.py) and the SCRAM interlock later (Phase 5) - DRY. 

5 

6Thresholds are directional and inclusive of the band edge: 

7 

8- ``warning_high`` / ``danger_high``: value at or above the threshold is 

9 WARNING / DANGER (metrics that fail high, e.g. core temperature). 

10- ``warning_low`` / ``danger_low``: value at or below the threshold is 

11 WARNING / DANGER (metrics that fail low, e.g. coolant flow). 

12 

13Unused sides are ``None``. ``coolant_pressure`` uses all four (fails both ways). 

14""" 

15
